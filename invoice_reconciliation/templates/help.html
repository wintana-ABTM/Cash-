How to Use This Tool

1. Prepare Your Files

You'll need two types of files:

  • Invoice PDFs - One or more invoice PDFs from your armored vendor (Loomis, Brinks, Garda, etc.)
  • Pickup CSV - A CSV file with your pickup records

2. CSV Format Requirements

Your pickup CSV should contain at minimum:

  • Location ID - Column can be named: location_id, store_id, site_id, store #, etc.
  • Pickup Date - Column can be named: pickup_date, date, service_date, etc.

Optional columns:

  • Amount - Expected pickup amount (amount, expected_amount, value, total)
  • Location Name - Human-readable name (location_name, store_name)
  • Pickup Type - Type of service (type, service_type, pickup_type)
  • Reference Number - Confirmation/ticket number

Example CSV:

location_id,location_name,pickup_date,pickup_type,expected_amount,reference_number
1001,Downtown Store,2024-01-05,Regular Pickup,1500.00,REF-001
1001,Downtown Store,2024-01-12,Regular Pickup,1750.50,REF-002
1002,Mall Location,2024-01-03,Regular Pickup,2200.00,REF-003
1002,Mall Location,2024-01-10,Regular Pickup,1900.25,REF-004

3. Upload and Run

  1. Go to the home page
  2. Upload your invoice PDF(s)
  3. Upload your pickup CSV
  4. Optionally filter by specific location IDs or months
  5. Click "Run Reconciliation"

4. Understanding Results

The tool compares invoices against pickups by location ID and month:

Status Meaning
Matched Invoice count matches pickup count for that location-month
Invoice Over More invoice line items than pickup records (potential overbilling)
Invoice Under Fewer invoice line items than pickup records (potential underbilling)
Mismatch General discrepancy detected

5. Download Results

After reconciliation, you can download:

  • Results CSV - Complete reconciliation results for all location-months
  • Unmatched Items CSV - List of invoice items and pickups that couldn't be matched

Supported Vendors

The tool auto-detects and parses invoices from:

  • Loomis
  • Brinks
  • Garda
  • Dunbar
  • Rochester Armored
  • Other vendors with standard invoice formats

Troubleshooting

"Could not parse any invoices"

The PDF format may not be recognized. Ensure the PDF contains selectable text (not scanned images). Try opening the PDF and copying text to verify.

"Could not identify location ID column"

Rename your CSV column to one of the recognized names: location_id, store_id, site_id, store #, etc.

"No pickup records found"

Check that your CSV has data rows below the header, and that dates are in a recognized format (YYYY-MM-DD, MM/DD/YYYY, etc.)
